## Rules of adjustment

- Ceiling "easy" or "rest": replace the session by an easy one (zone 1-2, half the duration) or rest.
  Never make up a skipped session the next day.
- Ceiling "moderate": keep the session, drop the intervals.
- Achilles pain above 3/10, or any symptom of illness: stop and report it before anything else.
- If two sessions in a row are replaced, the weekly review reduces the next week instead of catching up.
