Redesigned Fitness


Health and fitness aren’t one-size-fits-all, and your Fitness tab should reflect that. We’ve redesigned Fitness to bring your training plans, Cardio and Muscular Load, and progress together, with the flexibility to choose what you want to see.


If you’re training for a race, you might want to keep your running distance and pace front and center. If you’re focused on building strength, you might follow your Bench Press 1RM progression and Muscle Freshness. You can now pin the charts that matter to you, reorder sections, and hide what you don’t need.


Training Calendar


The new Training Calendar brings all your historical and planned activities together. Open it from the top left of Fitness to review your history, see upcoming workouts, and filter by activity type or metrics like distance and elevation.


•

Create Your First Plan: Bevel Intelligence will guide new users through creating a plan around their goals. For the first time, free users can try this too!


•

Bring Your Existing Plan: Already have a plan from Bevel Intelligence? You can migrate it to Fitness and continue making adjustments through chat or scheduled check-ins.


•

Manage Planned Activities: Find upcoming workouts in Today’s Activity and the Training Calendar. Start them on Apple Watch, mark them as skipped, or link them to completed activities. Workouts started from a planned activity, or completed workouts with the same activity type, will link automatically.


Muscular Load & Muscle Freshness


We know how much you’ve enjoyed Cardio Load, so we wanted to bring the same concept to your muscles. We’re introducing Muscular Load and Muscle Freshness to help you understand how your muscles are responding to training, both overall and by muscle group.


•

Muscular Load: Tracks the load on your muscles across strength and cardio workouts, using details like weight, reps, sets, and perceived effort (RPE) for Strength Builder, and muscle groups per activity type for cardio activities. Similar to Cardio Load, it compares your short-term and long-term load, with statuses like Productive, Maintaining, and Overtraining. For example, your biceps may show Overtraining while your Hamstrings show Detraining, helping you spot muscles you may be overworking or neglecting. Each muscle requires 1-2 workouts per week in the past 6 weeks to calibrate.


•

Muscle Freshness: Shows how recovered each muscle group is, with statuses like Recovered, Fatigued, and Depleted. It uses your training history and load over time to learn your personal recovery baseline. For example, after a run, your legs might be Fatigued while your upper body remains Recovered, helping you decide what to focus on next. Each muscle requires three workouts in recent training to calibrate. 


Explore both metrics by muscle group, or use the Muscle Map to see where your training is concentrated and which muscles may need more attention. Bevel Intelligence can also use these insights to guide your training plans.


Routines


Templates are now called Routines. Strength and cardio routines live together in Fitness, where you can manage, schedule, or start them directly on your Apple Watch. You can also create, edit, and start Routines directly from Bevel Intelligence.


We’ve also added 200+ new exercises you’ve asked for to the Strength Builder library. If an exercise is still missing, you can always ask Bevel Intelligence to create a custom one.


Customizable Charts


See how far you’ve come, and keep your next goal in focus. Fitness charts now give you more ways to explore your progress and pin the activities and metrics that matter most to you.


•

Activity Charts: Duration, distance, elevation, energy, pace, speed, heart rate, power, and cadence, depending on the activity.


•

Cardio Charts: VO₂ Max, Heart Rate Recovery, Cardio Focus, and a new Heart Rate Zones chart.


•

Exercise Charts: Estimated 1 Rep Max, Heaviest Weight, Volume, Sets, Reps, and Longest Duration for timed exercises.


•

Muscle Charts: Volume, Sets, Muscular Load, and Muscle Freshness.


We’ve also added a Year to Date option, cumulative and segmented views, and options to aggregate data by day or week.


Tap Edit in the Cardio or Strength section to manage your pinned charts, or pin a view directly from a chart’s menu. Each pin saves your selected metric and settings, so you can return to that exact view anytime.


Activity Details


We’ve redesigned Activity Details with more metrics, refreshed charts, and more detailed workout breakdowns.


•

Charts & Maps: Scrub through your workout to see metrics together and follow your position on the map. We’ve also added a cadence chart, satellite view, and chart reordering.


•

Running Form: New charts for vertical oscillation, stride length, and ground contact time, which Bevel Intelligence can use to better understand your running form and efficiency.


•

Splits & Intervals: Switch between split distances and see metrics like power and cadence for each split or interval.


•

Training Metrics: Added Muscular Load and muscle breakdowns, alongside refreshed Cardio Load, Heart Rate, Heart Rate Zones, Cardio Focus, and Heart Rate Recovery views.


•

Multisport Workouts: See the full workout, including transitions, then open each activity for more detail.


Personal Records


All that progress deserves to be celebrated. Personal Records now track your best performances across strength and cardio.


•

Strength: Heaviest Weight, Estimated 1 Rep Max, Session Volume, Set Volume, Set Reps, Lowest Assistance and Longest Time. 


•

Running: Longest Run and Fastest Time for various distances from 5k to a marathon.


•

Cycling: Longest Ride, Most Elevation Gain, Fastest Time for various distances, and Best Power over 5 seconds to 20 minutes. 


•

Swimming: Longest Swim and Fastest Time across both pool and open water distances.


Find your records within the corresponding activity or exercise chart, and see new achievements in Activity Details. Tap any record to view your top 10 efforts.


All-New Timeline


Timeline brings your activities, sleep, food, and other daily events into one chronological view. You can quickly log throughout the day, see how everything fits together, and plan meals ahead of time. To add the Timeline, tap Edit Home > Add Card > Timeline. 


What You’ll See

•

Pinned Metrics: Pin your favorite scores, macros, and micronutrients to the top of Timeline. For nutrition, you can switch views between consumed and remaining amounts and view your goals by weight or percentage.


•

Grouped Food Logs: Foods logged at the same time are grouped together, so you can see the meal’s macros at a glance or tap in for full nutrition facts.


•

Future Logging: Log foods for future dates to plan upcoming meals directly in Bevel.


•

Copy Foods: Copy foods to today, tomorrow, or multiple days at once.


•

Food Log Editing: Change the date or time, select multiple foods, and turn selected foods into a recipe.


•

Swipe to Delete: Quickly delete food logs and activities.


We’re continuing to expand the Nutrition Database, with thousands of foods already added and more on the way. Please keep reporting foods and submitting custom foods when something is missing.

All-New Bevel Intelligence


We've rebuilt Bevel Intelligence from the ground up. It's more capable, more personal, and now works proactively in the background, so you spend less time digging for answers and more time acting on them. 


Here's what's new:


Files


Files replaces Memory with a dynamic, persistent store of everything Bevel Intelligence knows about you: your preferences, goals, training plans, and generated artifacts. Files are automatically created, updated, and deleted as things change, so your context stays current.


Personalities


You can now choose how Bevel Intelligence communicates and coaches you, with four options:


•

Data Nerd: Analytical and detail-oriented, focused on trends, patterns, and the why behind your numbers.


•

Guardian: Calm and balanced, helping you zoom out and make decisions with your long-term health in mind.


•

Friend: Warm and encouraging, keeping you motivated and consistent.


•

Commander: Direct and action-oriented, clear on what matters and what to do next.


Personalities apply across conversations and embedded messages throughout the app. You can also give Bevel Intelligence more tone guidance, and it will adapt.


Check-ins


Bevel Intelligence no longer waits for you to ask a question. Check-ins let you schedule proactive insights and updates, such as:


•

A morning report with last night’s stats


•

Reminders to log meals or supplements


•

Daily progress check-ins for goals like marathon training, strength training, or nutrition


•

Weekly or monthly summaries with top insights from your data


Artifacts


Bevel Intelligence can now generate custom visual artifacts from your health data, helping you explore relationships between metrics like:


•

HRV vs. menstrual cycle phase


•

Sugar impact on deep sleep


Food Logging


Food logging is now conversational. Send a photo or describe what you ate, and Bevel Intelligence will log it directly. If a food isn’t in our database, Bevel creates a custom food on the spot. Follow-up edits are easy too; say “I only ate half of that,” and the portion updates immediately.


Training Plans


Bevel Intelligence can now build a complete training plan around your goal, whether it’s a 5K, marathon, or strength PR, and track your progress along the way. If life gets in the way, just tell Bevel, and it will reorganize the plan around you.


Strength Templates


You can now ask Bevel Intelligence to build custom strength templates and link them to your training plan. Bevel calculates weights and reps based on your history and preferences. Edit templates in natural language anytime, then start the workout from Bevel Intelligence or save it for later.


Biological Age


Biological Age is finally here. It’s the most powerful metric we’ve built, and we’ve redesigned the Biology experience around it. Biological Age surfaces the physiology, habits, and bloodwork that show how your body is truly aging.


Weekly Age Update


Each Monday, you’ll see a new Biological Age based on a rolling average of your past month of data. Tap into the weekly change to see which biomarkers are moving the needle and view a 20-year projection.


Age Biomarkers


Biological Age is powered by metrics across five categories:


•

Sleep: Time Asleep and Sleep Consistency


•

Activity: Daily Steps, Weekly Zone 2–3 and Zone 4-5 time, and Weekly Strength Training time


•

Fitness: VO2 Max, Resting Heart Rate, and Lean Body Mass


•

Lifestyle: Nutrition Score, Smoking Impact, and Alcohol


•

Blood Biomarkers: 9 core lab values integrated using Levine's PhenoAge model — Upload these via Health Records to unlock the full picture


Confidence Level


Confidence reflects the freshness and completeness of your data. As lab results age or data becomes stale, Bevel shifts weight toward your most recent physiological signals and is transparent about what’s missing.


Other Biomarkers


Your existing Biology metrics, like HRV baselines, resting heart rate, weight, and body fat, are still here with a cleaner design.


Health Records


Your most important health data is often the most fragmented, scattered across portals, PDFs, and paper records. That changes with Health Records.


You can now upload blood tests and health documents directly into Bevel. Lab results, clinical notes, imaging reports, prescriptions — your medical history now lives alongside the rest of your health data.


How It Works


From Bevel Intelligence or Biology, you can upload a file or take a photo from your phone or desktop.


Bevel automatically extracts your biomarkers and maps them to your Biology tab. For other documents, like clinical notes or immunization records, Bevel Intelligence can use them as context to personalize your guidance.


Privacy


We use industry-standard security practices, never sell or share your data with third parties, and ensure all data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Read our Privacy Policy or watch our video to learn more.

Nutrition Score


We've redesigned the Nutrition Score to give you a clearer, more accurate view of how your daily food choices support your health. The new approach is grounded in leading evidence-based nutrition research from AHEI, USDG, EU, and WHO guidelines, reflecting the patterns shown to reduce chronic disease risk. 


Instead of scoring each food individually, you'll now see Food Quality Contributors like vegetables, whole grains, or red meat. These contributors add or subtract from your daily score so you can see at t aglance how your choices are shaping your overall diet. 


Why We Made the Change


You shared that the previous score sometimes felt unclear or didn't reflect the true quality of your meals. It leaned heavily on macros, sodium and sugar, which meant certain foods were over- or under-valued. 


The new Nutrition Score better captures what actually matters: the quality of ingredients, the balance of your day, and the eating patterns linked to long-term health. To reinforce that balance, the score now includes diminishing returns so no single food group can carry your entire day.


How It Works


Each day starts with a locked score. After your first meal, a score from 1-100 appears and updates as you eat. Foods can move the score up, down, or stay neutral, depending on how they fit within the evidence‑based guidelines. For example, about 2.5 cups of vegetables can earn up to +10 points while more than one serving of processed meat can subtract up to −10 points.


If you track glucose, Glucose Impact continues to contribute to your Nutrition Score.


Positive Contributors


These Contributors will add to your Nutrition Score:


•

Vegetables (+10): Whole, minimally processed vegetables are strongly linked to lower risk of heart disease, diabetes, and cancer.


•

Fruits (+10): Whole fruits provide fiber and antioxidants that protect against chronic disease and support metabolic health.


•

Whole grains (+10): High-fiber whole grains improve gut health and lower risk of heart disease and diabetes.


•

Healthy oils (+10): Unsaturated fats from healthy oils reduce inflammation, support hormones, and protect cardiovascular health.


•

Nuts & Legumes (+10): Rich in plant proteins and healthy fats, nuts and legumes improve heart health and longevity.


•

Omega-3s (+10): Omega-3 fats support heart, brain, and metabolic health and lower inflammation.


Negative Contributors


These Contributors will subtract from your Nutrition Score:


•

Red meat (-5): High intake increases risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and certain cancers.


•

Processed meat (-10): Processed meats raise risk of heart disease, diabetes, and cancer due to sodium and preservatives.


•

Alcohol (-10): Even moderate alcohol use increases risk of cancer, hypertension, and liver disease.


•

Excess sugar (-10): Added sugars increase risk of obesity, insulin resistance, and cardiovascular disease.


•

Excess sodium (-5): Too much sodium raises blood pressure and risk of stroke and heart disease.

Heart Rate Recovery


At the bottom of the Activity page, you'll find a new Heart Rate Recovery Chart (HRR). HRR is an important indicator of cardiovascular health and fitness. In clinical settings, this is typically measured on a treadmill, where the participant reaches a peak heart rate and then stops immediately. HRR is then measured at the 1-minute and 2-minute marks post-exercise.


In practice, most wearables can only estimate your HRR, as most people don't immediately stop exercising after a workout (they might walk to cool down) or reach a peak HR that would make HRR measurements valid. Bevel estimates this by calculating the peak HR in the last 2 minutes of your workout and comparing it with your heart rate 2 minutes after that.


Please note that your HRR ranges are standardized based on your sex and age.

Energy Bank

What is Energy Bank?


Energy Bank measures your cumulative energy level throughout the day. It combines Recovery, Sleep, Strain, and Stress into one metric.


While Recovery tells you how well you "recharged" after a night of sleep, it doesn't tell you how you progress throughout the day. For example, taking naps or resting midday should, in theory, "recharge" your energy level, but the current Recovery score doesn't factor them in. Similarly, it's hard to tell the impacts of strenuous exercise on your Recovery midday. That's where Energy Bank comes in.


How does Energy Bank work?


Energy Bank is based on stress levels. Higher stress drains more energy, while lower stress drains less/recharge your Energy Bank. Typically, a good night of sleep/high recovery will recharge your Energy Bank more than poor sleep/low recovery.


Please note that the amount at which your Energy Bank recharge will not always be 1:1 with your Recovery score. Energy Bank gets exponentially harder to charge and drain towards the upper and lower ends. Lastly, it's important to understand that Energy Bank is cumulative. Your energy value of the previous day will be carried forward into the next day as the starting point.


Oh, and Energy Bank also comes with beautiful widgets and complications.
